      Is it real wood?

      Yes. Oak and walnut pieces are solid red oak and solid walnut. Dark stain is walnut-stained poplar, and the matte black and white finishes are painted poplar. The cologne steps are made from veneer plywood, which stays flat and stable across wide steps.

      How long will my order take?

      Every piece is built by hand after you order, so it isn't sitting in a warehouse waiting to ship. We'll email you when it's on its way. If you need it by a certain date, email us before ordering and we'll let you know if we can make it.

      Which material should I choose?

      Oak is the toughest and the most forgiving for everyday use. Walnut is our most premium wood, with a deep color that warms as it ages. Dark stain gets the walnut look for less. Matte black makes LED light pop, and matte white blends into light walls.

      Use Compare materials in the custom studio to see the price of each for your exact design.

      How is the LED shelf mounted?

      On a hidden wall bracket, so the shelf looks like it floats. The LED strip sits underneath and lights the wall below.

      Can I order a size the studio doesn't offer?

      Often, yes. Email us with the size or idea you have in mind and we'll tell you what's possible.

      How do I care for it?

      Dust with a soft, dry cloth. Oiled oak and walnut can be lightly sanded and re-oiled if they get scratched. Painted finishes wipe clean with a damp cloth.

      What does shipping cost?

      Shipping is free on orders over $200. Below that, it’s added at checkout. We currently ship within the US.
